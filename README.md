# QueueWise

QueueWise is an appointment booking and service management platform for small service businesses such as bicycle repair workshops, salons, garages, tutors, and wellness providers.

The application helps businesses manage their services, staff members, customers, and appointments in one place. It prevents booking conflicts by ensuring that appointments are assigned only to qualified staff members and that staff members are not booked for overlapping time periods.

## Phase 1 Features

- Manage business services, including name, description, duration, price and active status
- Manage staff members and their active/inactive status
- Assign services to qualified staff members
- Manage customer records
- Create appointments for customers
- Automatically calculate planned appointment end times from service duration
- Prevent staff double-booking and overlapping appointments
- View appointments by customer and staff member
- Update appointment status: `BOOKED`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`, and `NO_SHOW`
- Preserve appointment history by cancelling rather than deleting appointments

## Example Business Scenario

The initial demo scenario is **CycleFix Workshop**, a bicycle repair business. An administrator creates services such as puncture repair, brake adjustment and bicycle servicing, assigns them to qualified mechanics, and manages customer appointments.

The design is generic, so QueueWise can later support salons, garages, tutors, fitness providers, clinics and other appointment-based service businesses.
